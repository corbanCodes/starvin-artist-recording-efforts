Ivie Lamont

"The fast-food mindset is killing music. We cook with real ingredients."

Ivie has been in music and hip hop since he was a kid — long before it was a business. Based in Atlanta, he works at the intersection of deep music theory and modern production, where the writing actually holds up and the record still knocks.

Most of the industry is microwaving tracks. Starvin' Artist Recording Efforts exists to do the opposite: take the time, learn the craft, and put out music that's worth waiting for.

— Ivie Lamont
